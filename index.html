Fixed rules from the document: flee / pursuit = contested d20 + (Martial + Intrigue) ÷ 6; pursuer adds Crow's Nest +3 and +2 if its fleet is only longships / Swan / Iron; the fleeing side adds Ironwood Oars +3; Swan Ship adds +3 both ways. Difference > 5: caught (battle continues next round); −1 to 5: one attack then escape; otherwise escape. Each side may attempt to flee once per battle, after a round's damage. Capture is rolled once at the end of the battle: 1d10 per defeated hull, pooled by type. Ships that escape are not rolled. The fleeing or destroyed side does not capture. Swan Ship: 10% to dodge an entire enemy turn. Damage is pooled. Kill order is always split: the dromond is last; if both longships and galleys remain, half the damage is assigned to each (odd point to galleys); leftover overflows to remaining escorts, then the dromond. Capture: a natural 10 is a prize; Boarding Ramps also captures on 9. Dragons cannot be captured. Current (ex-j3): simultaneous accuracy, no engagement cap, no brace.
