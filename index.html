Fixed rules from the document: initiative = d20 + (Martial + Stewardship) ÷ divisor + traits (Sailor +1/level, Reckless ±d4, Organizer +1, Seastone Chair +2, Bloodvyne +1, Riptide +1, Myrish Eye +2). Flee / pursuit = contested d20 + (Martial + Intrigue) ÷ 6; pursuer adds Crow's Nest +3 and +2 if its fleet is only longships / Swan / Iron; the fleeing side adds Ironwood Oars +3; Swan Ship adds +3 both ways. Difference > 5: caught (battle continues next round); −1 to 5: one attack then escape; otherwise escape. Each side may attempt to flee once per battle, after a round's damage — the table asks surrender / flee / keep fighting once remaining hulls are posted. Capture is rolled once at the end of the battle (flee, annihilation, or after a failed/partial pursuit): 1d10 per defeated hull, pooled by type. Ships that escape are not rolled. The fleeing or destroyed side does not capture. If the enemy declines to pursue, remaining ships escape and then the standing side rolls capture on wrecks. Swan Ship: 10% to dodge an entire enemy turn. Damage is pooled. v3 kill order: longships, then galleys, then the dromond. Current / table (naval admin): the dromond is always last; if both longships and galleys remain, half the damage is assigned to each (odd point to galleys); leftover from a type that is wiped overflows to remaining escorts, then the dromond. Each defeated hull is captured with 1d10 per ship (pool by type, the way Dice Maiden is used at the table): a natural 10 is a prize; Boarding Ramps also captures on a 9. Captured ships do not fight for the capturer this battle. Dragons cannot be captured.
